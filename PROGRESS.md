## p8 Mock review panel — ROUND 1 COMPLETE
Two reviewers ran with fresh context and artifacts only. Both recommended major
revision. `issues.json` holds the full ledger: 19 findings, 17 fixed or in

The panel independently verified four things, which is worth recording: the
BepiPred-3.0 baseline row recomputed exactly, both nested-fusion results
reproduced bit-exactly from the shipped module, and no label leakage was found
in the leave-one-sequence-out loop.

What it found that changed the paper's conclusions:

1. **R2-001 (fatal).** Every routing interval resampled seeds on a fixed
   15-antigen split, measuring re-run dispersion rather than sampling
   uncertainty over antigens. Antigen-level intervals are roughly ten times
   wider. Fixed with a two-way bootstrap over antigens and seeds
   (`policy_resampling.py`, 2000 resamples). **Most routing quality claims did
   not survive**: adaptive-split versus always-local falls from a decisive
   ΔMCC +0.034 (p=0.0039) to +0.025 [−0.011, +0.065], with only F1 separating
   them. The latency and disclosure gains survive comfortably.
2. **R2-002 (major).** The "no quality difference versus always-remote" claim
   rested on MCC while the paper's own macros showed ROC-AUC and AP intervals
   excluding zero. All four metrics are now reported for every comparison. Under
   antigen-level resampling all four contain zero, so the claim is now honest
   but is a statement about power, not equivalence.
3. **R2-003 (major).** The stated carbon mechanism was wrong: the operational
   term is 7.0e-06 gCO2e locally against per-request totals near 0.12, so
   carbon tracks device-request counts, not compute time. Corrected, with the
   closed-form break-even routed fraction added.
4. **R2-004 (major).** The six-decimal replication claim had never executed.
   Now run: 44 grid rows, max deviation 4.9e-07, recorded in the artifact.
5. **R2-006 (major).** The per-fold threshold was the quantile of the held-out
   antigen's own scores — transductive, though not label leakage. Fixed to fit
   on training folds; this also dissolved the 39-of-47 tie degeneracy R2-005
   flagged, which fell to 8 of 47.
6. **R3-001/003 (major).** The carbon inversion and the nested-selection
   correction are both instances of known results (Kumar & Lu 2010; Varma &
   Simon 2006; Jelizarow et al. 2010). Cited, and both contributions reframed
   from discovery to domain instantiation.
7. **R3-005 (major).** Missing 2024-2026 conformational predictors added.

Every reference the panel suggested was verified against Crossref before use.

## Runtime (measured, 2026-10-25)

The GPU is not the constraint on this pipeline and buying a faster one would be
wasted money. From the run artifacts, each seed spends about 330 s in the edge
tree ensemble against 1.5 s in the local model, so the 10-seed sweep is roughly
77 minutes of CPU that no accelerator touches. The GPU is needed only for a
one-time extraction of frozen ESM-2 features, which are cached per checkpoint
under `outputs/feature_cache/<checkpoint>/`.

That ensemble was fitted with `n_jobs=1`. It is now `FIT_N_JOBS`, resolved from
`EDGECONBCE_N_JOBS` and defaulting to all cores. Scikit-learn forests are
deterministic given `random_state` regardless of worker count, and this was
checked rather than assumed: fitting 240 trees on an 80k-row problem at
`n_jobs=1` and `n_jobs=-1` gave a maximum probability difference of exactly
`0.00e+00`, with a 1.9x speed-up on two cores. Setting `EDGECONBCE_N_JOBS=1`
reproduces the earlier artifacts exactly.

Practical consequence: choose the runtime with the most vCPUs, not the fastest
GPU. A free T4 instance and an H100 instance finish this job within a couple of
minutes of each other.

## Blockers — both resolved 2026-10-27

**CAR-001 — carbon constants: RESOLVED, not an error.** The embodied term is
charged at a device-utilisation fraction, `0.20` locally and `0.65` remotely,
applied as literals at the call site in `edgeconbce_real_benchmark_v2.py` and
recorded in no configuration file. 0.600 x 0.20 = 0.120 and 0.2125 x 0.65 =
0.138125, which is exactly what the artifacts hold; recomputing every
per-antigen total from the recorded configuration plus these fractions matches
the stored values to 0e+00 gCO2e across all 47 antigens checked. That is why
the two discrepancies were not a common factor.

The fractions are now documented in the manuscript's cost-accounting section
and written into the artifact by `real_benchmark.py`. Because the embodied term
dominates every per-request total, the manuscript also reports a sensitivity:
of five alternative fraction settings, four preserve the ordering and one
(local 0.10, edge 0.90) would move the break-even routed fraction to 0.690 and
make routing the greener option. The conclusion is robust for the hardware pair
modelled, and the limitation now says so precisely. Lifting the fractions into
the YAML config is a follow-up; it was not done here because the pipeline
constructs devices in more than one place and the change could silently alter
existing results.

**R3-007 — IEDB provenance: RESOLVED.** The subset derives from
`data/external/IEDBSeqsNotSharedAt20ID.fasta`, a companion collection of 3560
IEDB sequences selected not to share more than 20% identity with the BP3 data,
restricted to 45-595 residues. All 252 subset sequences (205 train, 47 test)
are present verbatim in that source. Independence was then measured directly
against all 358 BP3C50ID sequences: 0 shared identifiers, 0 shared sequences,
and a maximum 10-mer containment of 0.000 — not one antigen shares even a
single 10-residue run. The "two independent datasets" claim is sound.

The artifact's `data_sources` block had hardcoded the DTU BP3C50ID names and
URLs regardless of which FASTA files a run actually used, which is what made
the record self-contradictory. `edgeconbce_real_benchmark_v2.py` now derives
them from the configured paths and does not claim DTU provenance for files that
are not DTU files. Verified on both dataset configurations.

One item remains unrecorded: how the 252 antigens were sampled from the 2203
source sequences inside the length bound. The inputs are reproducible; the
sampling is not. It is flagged in the limitations.

## Superseded blocker text

**CAR-001 — the carbon constants cannot be reproduced from the stored
configuration.** The operational term reproduces exactly; the embodied term,
which dominates both totals, does not. The configuration declares 0.6000 gCO2e
per local request and 0.2125 per remote request; the recorded per-antigen
totals imply 0.1200 and 0.1381. The two discrepancies are not a common factor,
so this is not a unit error. The per-policy ordering is unaffected, because it
follows from the cost identity for any positive constants, but the absolute
figures are not currently reproducible. `carbon_decomposition.py` performs this
check on every run.

**R3-007 — the IEDB subset's provenance is internally inconsistent.** Its
provenance block names the DTU BP3C50ID files while pointing at IEDB subset
paths. The fusion result's second dataset rests on it.
